# Franchise Retention Experience V1

This layer gives returning players a reason to resume and creates visible goals beyond simply
simulating the schedule.

## Included experiences

- **Front Office Briefing** — a dynamic welcome-back headline using the active phase, record,
  recent form, roster, save time, and current franchise face.
- **GM Challenge Paths** — Balanced, Builder, Win Now, and Deal Maker paths change the featured
  seasonal objective without changing or biasing simulation results. The choice persists across
  section navigation and app restarts through the existing lightweight UI-preference file.
- **Season Quest Board** — four objectives update automatically from real games, wins, rotations,
  trades, signings, ages, and phase progression.
- **Milestone Cabinet** — Prepared, Tip-Off, First Win, Deal Maker, Seasoned, and Champion badges
  unlock only when the durable franchise history supports them.
- **Around Your Universe** — a compact live feed summarizing the latest result, the league leader,
  and current roster availability.
- **Contextual CTA** — each GM path routes the player toward the most relevant existing workspace.

The retention hub appears after the Rookie GM walkthrough has been completed or skipped, keeping
the first-session experience focused. All information is derived read-only from existing franchise
and trade state. The module does not save, commit, advance time, or alter simulation outcomes.
