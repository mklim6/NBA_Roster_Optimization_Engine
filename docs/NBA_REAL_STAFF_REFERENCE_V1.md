# NBA Real Staff Reference V1

As-of date: **September 7, 2026**.

V1 seeds the **real head coach for all 30 NBA teams** into Franchise Mode. The six 2026 offseason changes are Chicago (Tiago Splitter), Dallas (Dusty May), Milwaukee (Taylor Jenkins), New Orleans (Jamahl Mosley), Orlando (Sean Sweeney), and Portland (Micah Nori).

Supporting roles (lead assistant, development coach, lead scout, and medical/performance director) remain deterministic simulator-generated personnel for now. This avoids inventing a one-to-one real-world title where NBA organizations use different staff structures and where public staff/contract information is incomplete.

## Simulation safety policy

Real-world identity fields:
- Head coach name
- Head coach birth date / age as of Sep. 7, 2026

Simulator fields retained:
- Ratings
- Traits
- Experience value
- Contract years
- Salary

Existing saves are migrated in-place the next time the staff state is ensured. The head-coach identity changes to the verified real-world coach, while every gameplay rating and team staff effect is preserved exactly.

## Verification basis

- NBA.com 2026 Coaching Tracker and NBA.com overview of the six new coaches for 2026-27.
- Basketball-Reference 2025-26 NBA Coaches for incumbents carried into 2026-27.
- NBA Coaches Association / Basketball-Reference / coach biographies for birth dates.
