# Franchise Onboarding + Visual Progression V2

V2 replaces the original all-at-once help wall with a four-screen **Rookie GM walkthrough**.
New users learn one idea at a time: the next-action system, the season loop, action safety,
and the main ways to begin. They can skip the tour or replay it from the sidebar at any time.

The persistent **Season Control** HUD uses live franchise data to show:

- the current season stage and completed stages;
- league schedule progress and unresolved inbox decisions;
- the one recommended action that moves the franchise forward;
- direct navigation to that action and Team Management.

Every workspace masthead now includes the active team logo, section-specific color, contextual
tool chips, and a green/yellow/red action-risk label. Green screens are safe to explore, yellow
screens can change the team after confirmation, and red screens can advance league time.

The presentation layer is responsive, keyboard-focus visible, and respects reduced-motion
preferences. It reuses the project’s existing real team logos and player headshots rather than
introducing generic artwork.

This work is presentation-only. It does not alter checkpoints, franchise state, simulations,
transactions, free agency, draft logic, CBA logic, or active saves.
